FAQ

Why is there no live time for my train or metro? Atac and Trenitalia do not publish live data for these, so the app shows scheduled times.

Why do I only see "n" lines? Between about 00:30 and 05:30 only night buses run in Rome.

Data sources: Roma Mobilità open data; Trenitalia timetables via deryclem/trenitalia-gtfs (CC BY 4.0). Prossima Fermata is not affiliated with Atac, Roma Mobilità or Trenitalia.
