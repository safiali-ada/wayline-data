Prossima Fermata – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Prossima Fermata ("Fermata") is made by Syed Safi Ali. It does not collect, store or share personal data.

  • No account, no ads, no tracking. There are no analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Location is used only on your iPhone to show nearby stops, plan trips and give live directions. It is never sent to us or anyone else. During live directions the app may use location in the background until the trip ends.
  • Favourites and recent places are stored only on your iPhone and are deleted if you delete the app.
  • Network requests: the app downloads public timetable and real-time data from Roma Mobilità and GitHub. These requests contain no personal identifiers; the servers may log your IP address as any web server does.
  • Notifications are created on the device for get-off and arrival alerts.

Questions: safialicr@gmail.com

Italiano

Prossima Fermata non raccoglie, conserva né condivide dati personali. La posizione è usata solo sull’iPhone per mostrare le fermate vicine e guidare il viaggio e non viene mai inviata. Preferiti e luoghi recenti restano sul dispositivo. Nessuna pubblicità, nessun tracciamento.
